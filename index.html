The State of the Planet · 2025

Seven of nine.

For eleven thousand years, nine planetary boundaries held the stable world civilization was built on. As of 2025, seven are breached — climate, biosphere, land, freshwater, nutrient cycles, novel entities, and now the oceans. Only two remain safe.

    A boundary can be crossed. The ozone layer proves it can also be pulled back. The window is still open.

    Where We Fight

    Four of those seven are fought in the earth itself.

    Novel entities

    Chemical pollution, plastics, and contamination accumulating in land and water.

    Land-system change

    Ground degraded, converted, poisoned, and abandoned.

    Biosphere integrity

    Biodiversity collapsing faster than any time in human history.

    Biogeochemical flows

    The nitrogen and phosphorus cycles broken by how we grow and waste.

    We don't offset them. We build companies to reverse them.

    Dead ground in. Clean firm power and living systems out.

    How it works

    We pre-sell future power to anchor buyers — data centers, industrials, utilities — before we build. That bankable demand unlocks project finance at the asset level. Public and grant capital carries the land cleanup. We keep strategic control of every megawatt: defined-term contracts, dedicated blocks, expansion rights — anchor buyers never own the whole plant.

    Site screen — three gates

    1. 01A bankable geothermal resource is actually present
    2. 02The site is genuinely acquirable — not NPL/CERCLA-encumbered
    3. 03Nearby load isn't already locked up
